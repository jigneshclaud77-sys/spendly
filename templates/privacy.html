Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

01 Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect more than what is needed to provide the service.

02 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

03 Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records are permanently removed from our systems.

04 Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers, such as hosting and database services, solely to operate the app — these providers do not have independent rights to use your data.

05 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through the contact details provided in your account settings, and we'll be happy to help.
